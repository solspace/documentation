# Dashboard Widgets<Badge type="pro" text="Pro" />

Freeform Pro includes four widgets for Craft's **Dashboard**. Select **New widget** to add one, then configure its forms and display options. Forms are listed alphabetically in widget settings.

Chart widgets offer consistent size presets, submission totals, scrollable interactive legends, and helpful empty states. **Recent Submissions** lets you search, filter, sort, browse pages, and refresh entries. **Quick Form** uses Craft-style controls and displays submission confirmations directly in the widget.

<TOCInline toc={toc} />

## Linear Chart widget<Badge type="pro" text="Pro" /><Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.17.3+" />

Track submissions over time with a **line** or **bar** chart. Each form uses the color defined in its **Form Color** setting under **Settings → General** in the form builder.

The selected date range and submission total appear above the chart. Click a form in the legend to hide or show its series. Forms with no submissions in the selected period are omitted, and an empty-state message appears when there are no results.

<Photo
  img={require('@site/static/img/craft/freeform/v5/cp/widgets/linear.jpg')}
  alt="Linear Chart widget showing the date range, submission total, whole-number axis, and form legend"
/>

<details className="hidden-heading">
  <summary>Configuration Options</summary>
    - **Title** - Enter a custom widget title, or leave it blank to use the default.
    - **Chart Type** - Choose **Line** or **Bar**.
    - **Chart Size**<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.17.3+" /> - Choose **Compact**, **Normal**, or **Large** for the chart and legend. Defaults to **Normal**.
    - **Date Range** - Choose the period to display:
      - Last 24 hours
      - Last 7 days
      - Last 30 days
      - Last 60 days
      - Last 90 days
    - **Combine All Forms into One Series** - Show the combined submission counts from the selected forms as a single line or bar series.
    - **Include Spam Submissions**<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.15.24+" /> - Include submissions marked as spam in the chart.
    - **Forms** - Include **All** forms, or select specific forms to display.
</details>

## Radial Chart widget<Badge type="pro" text="Pro" /><Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.17.3+" />

Compare submission totals across forms with a **pie**, **donut**, or **polar area** chart. Each form uses the color defined in its **Form Color** setting under **Settings → General** in the form builder.

The selected date range and submission total appear above the chart. Click a form in the legend to hide or show its segment. Forms with no submissions in the selected period are omitted, and an empty-state message appears when there are no results.

## Recent Submissions widget<Badge type="pro" text="Pro" /><Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.17.3+" />

Browse recent submissions from all forms or a selected set. Each entry shows its status, form name, submission ID, and how long ago it was received. Click an entry to open its control panel details, or hover over the date to see the full date and time.

Search by title, form name, or ID, filter by form or status, and sort newest or oldest first. Use the page controls to browse results, **Refresh** to load the latest entries while keeping your selections, or **View all submissions** to open the full submission list.

Search, filters, sorting, and pagination apply to the latest **100 submissions** from the selected forms. Search does not include individual field answers. When pagination is disabled, the widget shows only the configured number of matching entries.

<Photo
  scale="60"
  img={require('@site/static/img/craft/freeform/v5/cp/widgets/recent.jpg')}
  alt="Recent Submissions widget with search, form and status filters, sorting, status indicators, relative dates, and Refresh"
/>

<details className="hidden-heading">
  <summary>Configuration Options</summary>
    - **Title** - Enter a custom widget title, or leave it blank to use the default.
    - **Submissions to Show** - Enter a number from `1` to `100` for the submissions displayed per page. When pagination is disabled, this limits the total displayed. Defaults to `5`.
    - **Show Search and Filters**<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.17.3+" /> - Show the search, form and status filters, and sorting controls. Enabled by default.
    - **Show Pagination**<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.17.3+" /> - Show page controls to browse the latest 100 submissions from the selected forms. Enabled by default.
    - **Forms** - Include **All** forms, or select specific forms to display.
</details>

## Quick Form widget<Badge type="pro" text="Pro" /><Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.17.3+" />

Add a Freeform form directly to Craft's **Dashboard**, such as a support request form for clients. Fields blend in with Craft's control panel styling.

After a successful submission, the widget displays **Submission received** and your configured success message. Select **Submit another** to start a fresh form. Forms with integration actions may need to complete additional steps before showing a confirmation.

:::warning
Some complex forms may not work as expected inside the widget.
:::

<Photo

  alt="Quick Form widget with Craft-style inputs, a searchable dropdown, range slider, summary, and drag-and-drop uploads"

<details className="hidden-heading">
  <summary>Configuration Options</summary>

  - **Title** - Enter a custom widget title, or leave it blank to use the default.
  - **Success Message** - Enter a message to display after a successful submission, or leave it blank to use the form's success message.
  - **Form** - Select the form to display in the widget.
</details>
