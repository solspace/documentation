# Stripe Payment<Badge type="pro" text="Pro" /><Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.0+" /> {#stripe-payment}


<span id="stripe-payment" />
When the [Stripe Payments](../../integrations/stripe-payments.mdx) integration is configured, a Stripe Payments special field type will become available. This field enables your forms to collect (but not store) credit card or other payment information and pass it off to Stripe.
