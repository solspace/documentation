# Managing Forms

The Forms dashboard page offers extensive customization options. You can choose to display forms as cards or in a list table, customize the color and icon of each form, set the display order of forms, create groups to organize forms, and archive forms that you don't want to appear on the dashboard.

## Creating a Form<Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.17.2+" />

Click **Add new form** and choose how to start:

- **[Guided setup](./guided-setup.mdx):** choose a purpose and configure fields, appearance, behavior, and emails through a seven-step wizard.
- **[Create with AI](./create-with-ai.mdx)**<Badge type="pro" text="Pro" />: describe the form or recreate one from compatible reference files using an enabled AI integration.
- **Quick setup:** enter the form details and choose a blank form or a suggested starting point, then continue in the builder.

On Craft 5, choosing one of the 12 starting points in **Guided setup** or **Quick setup** also assigns a matching default [Form Icon](./settings.mdx#general). You can change or remove it in **Settings → General** after creation. Blank forms start without an icon.

Administrators control these choices in [Form Creation Options](../../configuration/settings/general.mdx#form-creation-options). When only one method is enabled, **Add new form** opens it directly. AI availability also depends on the edition, permissions, and integration setup; an unavailable AI option can offer setup guidance to users who can configure integrations.

## Searching Forms<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.17.3+" /> {#searching-forms}

Use **Search** beside the cards/list view controls to filter forms as you type. Search matches the form's **name**, **handle**, and **description**, ignoring capitalization. When you enter multiple words, every word must appear somewhere in those details. The toolbar wraps on smaller screens, with larger touch targets on mobile.

The same search stays active when switching between Cards and List views. It also filters **Archived Forms** in both views; expand that panel to see matching archived forms. Groups without matching forms are hidden while searching.

Click **Clear search**, or press **Escape** while the search input is focused, to show all forms again. Card reordering is disabled while a search is active; clear the search before moving cards. Searching does not change the saved order or group assignments.

## Form Cards<Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.9+" />

Forms can be displayed as cards, and you can manage, reorder, and group them.

Each form card will display the form name, handle, and description, along with the statistics showing the number of submissions, the number of spam submissions, number of [saved forms](../save-continue-later.mdx) (if applicable) and a color-coded chart at the bottom of the card indicating submission counts over the past 30 days. Additionally, you will have the ability to reorder, archive, duplicate, or delete the form.

On Craft 5, forms with a [Form Icon](./settings.mdx#general) also show a tinted icon badge beside the form name, matching the form’s color.

### Reordering Cards<Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.17+" />

In **Lite** and **Pro**, users with permission to manage forms can reorder cards directly on the Forms dashboard. **Pro** also supports moving cards between groups, including **Other** and empty groups.

<VerticalStepWrapper>
<StepMarkdown>
Switch the Forms dashboard to **cards** view.
</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>
Use a card's **Move this Form Card** handle to drag it to a new position. In Pro, drop it into another group to change its group assignment.
</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>
Release the card. The new position and group assignment save automatically, with a **“Form position saved.”** Craft notification. If saving fails, the previous arrangement is restored and an error notification appears.
</StepMarkdown>
</VerticalStepWrapper>

For keyboard control, focus the card's move handle and use **Up/Down** to reorder within its group or **Left/Right** to move to an adjacent group. Moving to another group places the card at the end of that group. The dashboard and **Form groups** slideout use the same saved arrangement. Dashboard card moves save automatically; changes made inside the slideout require **Save changes**.

## Form List<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.6+" />

Forms can also be displayed as a table list, showing them in alphabetical order. The list does not display group headings or the custom card order. You can still open [Form groups](#form-groups) from the dashboard controls to manage assignments, then switch to cards view to see the groups.

Each row in the list will display the form name, handle, and description, along with the statistics showing the number of submissions, the number of spam submissions, and a color-coded chart indicating submission counts over the past 30 days. Additionally, you will have the ability to archive, duplicate, or delete the form.

On Craft 5, the first column displays the [Form Icon](./settings.mdx#general) in the form’s color, with no visible column heading. Forms without an icon have an empty cell. Craft 4 does not show this column.

### Create and Rename Groups

Click **Add group** to create an empty group and enter its name. To rename an existing group, click its name or pencil icon. Press **Enter** or click outside the input to finish editing; **Escape** restores the previous name.

Each group shows a count of its forms. Empty groups display **Drop forms here** so you can add forms immediately.

### Move Forms and Reorder Groups

- **Move or reorder forms:** drag a form row within its group or into another group. You can also drop it into the **Unassigned** section to remove its group assignment.
- **Move without dragging:** open the ellipsis button on a form row and choose a destination under **Move to**. This moves the form to the end of the selected group. Forms already in a group also offer **Unassigned** as a destination.
- **Reorder groups:** drag a group's move handle. For keyboard control, focus that handle and use **Up/Down**, or **Home/End** to move the group to the beginning or end.

The **Unassigned** section is collapsible and shows a count of forms without a group. Archived forms are excluded from the slideout. On the cards dashboard, unassigned forms appear under **Other** when groups are present.

You can also [move cards directly on the dashboard](#reordering-cards), including into empty groups.

### Remove a Group

Click the group's trash icon, then choose **Remove group** in the confirmation. Its forms move to **Unassigned**; removing a group does not delete its forms or submissions. Choose **Keep group** to cancel the removal.

### Save or Discard Changes

Click **Save changes** to apply group names, group order, form order, and assignments to the dashboard. The button becomes available when there are changes to save. **Cancel** or closing the slideout discards unsaved edits.

If saving fails, the slideout keeps your edits so you can retry. Unlike [dashboard card moves](#reordering-cards), changes inside this slideout do not save automatically.

## Archiving Forms<BadgeGroup><Badge type="lite" text="Lite" /><Badge type="pro" text="Pro" /></BadgeGroup><Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.5+" />

Archive old or unused forms with the Archive action on a form card or list row. Archiving removes the form from the active dashboard display; the form remains active on your website and can still be viewed and edited in the builder.

In cards view, expand **Archived Forms** at the bottom of the dashboard. Its count badge shows how many archived forms are available. The panel displays form details and localized archive dates, with **Restore** and delete actions.

Choose **Restore** to return a form to the active dashboard. Deleting a form is separate from archiving and also deletes its submissions.

## Errors & Update Notices

The top of the **Forms** dashboard page will conveniently let you know if something is wrong by displaying a banner indicating the number of errors that Freeform logged, if available, as well as [Update Notices](../../configuration/notices-alerts.mdx) to keep you informed about issues that may specifically affect your site.

:::info
We respect your privacy, so this feature only pulls update notices from the Solspace.com site and **does not and cannot send any information back**. Solspace collects **no** information at all. The comparison check happens on your Freeform site environment, not on the Solspace site. This feature can also be disabled in the plugin settings.
:::

## Create Forms with AI<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.15+" />

Describe the form you need, or in **Freeform 5.17+**, attach PDFs, images, or screenshots for AI to recreate with editable native fields. Generation uses an enabled AI integration; reference-file support depends on the provider and model.

Generation runs in the background, with progress checks that can be resumed by reopening **Add new form → Create with AI** in the same browser tab. When it finishes, choose **Open form** to review the saved result. Supported fillable PDFs also supply embedded dropdown choices to help recreate select fields accurately.

See [Create Forms with AI](./create-with-ai.mdx) for the workflow, supported integrations and file limits, queue setup, and what to review before publishing.
