# Guided Setup<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.17.2+" />

**Guided setup** walks you through creating a form without an AI integration. Choose a purpose, adjust the suggested fields, and configure the form before saving it. The resulting form uses the same builder and fields as other Freeform forms.

Open **Freeform → Forms → Add new form → Guided setup**. If your site only enables guided setup, **Add new form** opens it directly. Administrators can choose the available creation methods in [General Settings](../../configuration/settings/general.mdx#form-creation-options).

## Setup Steps

| Step | What to configure |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Choose a starting point such as Contact, Quote request, Application, Event registration, or Feedback. **Other** provides a flexible starting point. |
| **Details** | Set the form name, description, default submission status, and color. Expand **Advanced** to review the form handle and, when site-aware forms are enabled, the sites assigned to the form. |
| **Fields** | Rename fields, change their types, mark them required, edit choices, and arrange rows. Add or remove fields to suit the form. |
| **Appearance** | Choose the [formatting template](../../templates/formatting/index.mdx) used when the form is embedded on your site. |
| **Behavior** | Set what happens after submission, success and error messages, and whether submissions are stored in Freeform. |
| **Emails** | Optionally notify your team or send the visitor a confirmation. |
| **Preview** | Check the rendered form at Desktop, Tablet, or Phone size before choosing **Create form**. |

Use **Continue** and **Back** to move between steps. Completed steps can also be reopened from the step bar. Where available, **Switch to Form Preview** lets you inspect the form while configuring it. Preview submissions and uploads are disabled.

Nothing is saved until you choose **Create form**. **Cancel** leaves setup without creating a form; **Start over** resets the draft.

## Adjusting Fields

The selected purpose supplies an editable set of suggested fields. In **Fields**:

- Click a field name to rename it, or its type icon to choose another available field type.
- Toggle the required control beside a field to change whether visitors must complete it.
- Open **Edit choices** on a choice field and enter one choice per line.
- Use the **+** controls to insert another field. Drag fields together to make rows of up to four columns, or use the outer row handles to move a whole row.
- For keyboard reordering, focus a field's move handle and use **Up/Down** to reorder or **Left/Right** to join an adjacent row. **Shift+Up** moves it into its own row.

The available field types and settings follow your Freeform edition and the user's [Limited Users](../../configuration/limited-users.mdx) restrictions. File fields also require an available upload location. After creation, the full [Layout](./layout.mdx) editor provides additional field types and settings.

## Email Notifications

Enable **Notify my team** and provide the recipient address, or enable **Send the visitor a confirmation** and select an Email field from the form. A visitor confirmation requires an Email field.

Depending on your permissions and [notification template settings](../../configuration/settings/template-manager.mdx#email-templates), you can create a new template or select an existing one. New templates use the site's default sender settings and include the submitted fields. Template creation follows the configured global or form-specific scope and storage settings.

No email is sent during setup. After creation, review the templates and recipients in [Notifications](../email-notifications/index.mdx), then submit the actual embedded form to test delivery.

## Reviewing the Preview

The final step renders the selected formatting template. Use the device selector to check the layout before creating the form.

## After Creation

**Create form** saves the form and opens it in the builder. Review any further settings, conditional rules, or integrations, then use [Preview](./preview.mdx) and the [Embed Form](./settings.mdx#embed-form) guidance to prepare it for your site.
