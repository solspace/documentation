# Form Builder

<LegacyReferenceRedirect targets={legacyAnchors} />

Build simple or multi-page forms by arranging fields, choosing how submissions are handled, and connecting the services you use. Fields, notifications, rules, integrations, and per-form settings are configured together in the form builder.

## Overview

Use these areas as you configure a form:

| Area | What you configure |
| --- | --- |
| [Layout](./layout.mdx) | Add fields, edit their properties, and arrange rows and pages. The final front-end appearance comes from the formatting template. |
| [Notifications](../email-notifications/index.mdx) | Send to administrators, selected recipients, or addresses entered in Email fields. Four builder notification types are available, plus template-based notifications. |
| [Rules](../conditional-rules.mdx)<Badge type="pro" text="Pro" /> | Show or hide fields and groups, skip pages, or submit the form based on field values. |
| [Integrations](./integrations.mdx) | Enable configured connections and map form data to services or Craft elements. |
| [Settings](./settings.mdx) | Set the form's identity, data storage, submission behavior, limits, and schedule. |
| [Usage & Stats](./settings.mdx#usage-and-stats)<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.17.3+" /> | In Settings, review submission activity, configuration, Craft 5 element usage, and copyable form diagnostics. |
| [Preview](./preview.mdx) | Inspect the saved form, compare sample templates at different viewport sizes, and test conditional flow. |

### Navigation on Smaller Screens<Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.17+" />

When the builder's tabs do not fit, open **More** to reach the remaining sections. The active section stays visible in the tab bar, and **Save**, **Undo**, and **Redo** retain their space in the header. In narrow windows, navigation moves to a second row.

Selecting a section from **More** brings it into the tab bar. The menu also highlights validation errors in hidden sections. Use **Up/Down** to navigate the open menu and **Escape** to close it and return focus to **More**.

## Build and Publish a Form

<VerticalStepWrapper>
<StepMarkdown>
Open **Freeform → Forms → Add new form**, then choose [Guided setup](./guided-setup.mdx), [Create with AI](./create-with-ai.mdx), or [Quick setup](./managing-forms.mdx#creating-a-form). See [Managing Forms](./managing-forms.mdx) for dashboard controls and opening existing forms.
</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>
Arrange fields and pages in **Layout**, then select a field to edit its settings. Configure notifications, rules, integrations, and form settings as needed. See [Layout & Pages](./layout.mdx) for the complete editing workflow and multi-page limitations.
</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>
Save the form and check its appearance in [Preview](./preview.mdx), then use the [Embed Form guidance](./settings.mdx#embed-form) to display it on your site. Submit the actual form to test validation, notifications, integrations, and the success behavior.
</StepMarkdown>
</VerticalStepWrapper>
