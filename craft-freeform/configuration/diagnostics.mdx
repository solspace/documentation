# Diagnostics<Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.17+" />

Freeform's Diagnostics page helps you identify configuration issues, check your installation, and share useful information with Solspace Support. Open **Freeform → Settings → Diagnostics** in the Craft control panel.

Visit this page when setting up Freeform, after updating your site, or when troubleshooting a problem. It includes an **Overview** of your installation and a **Health Checks** tab for running more detailed checks.

:::tip
[Quick troubleshooting for common issues with your form's appearance, behavior, or submissions on the front end.](../guides/guide/troubleshooting-form-issues.mdx)
:::

## Overview

The Overview tab summarizes your server, Craft configuration, and Freeform settings:

- **Server Checks:** Freeform edition and version, available Freeform updates, Craft and PHP compatibility, database driver and version, operating system, memory and execution limits, upload limits, PHP sessions, extensions, and ModSecurity.
- **Site Settings:** Environment, Dev Mode, admin changes, multi-site configuration, async CSRF inputs, automatic queue processing, Craft upload limits, email configuration, and detected Blitz caching.
- **General Settings:** Submit-button behavior, automatic scrolling, duplicate-submission prevention, CSRF refresh, JavaScript insertion, session context, search indexing, queue usage, data purging, and site-aware forms.
- **Spam Controls:** Spam storage and behavior, blocking options, minimum submission time, form expiration, submission throttling, and bypass settings.
- **Templates:** Formatting, email, and success template directories and storage settings.
- **Notices & Alerts:** Developer Digest, critical error reports, update notices, logging level, and log entry counts with links to relevant logs.
- **Statistics:** Counts of forms, fields, favorite fields, submissions, spam submissions, templates, integrations, and form types.
- **Integrations, Form Types, and Modules:** A summary of integration usage, form types, and installed Craft modules.

A banner highlights potential issues or advisories. Status indicators help you interpret individual settings:

- 🟢 Green - Enabled / Valid
- ⚪️ Gray - Disabled
- 🟡 Yellow - Advisory
- 🔴 Red - Potential issue

:::info
A disabled setting is not necessarily a problem. Review the accompanying explanation to decide whether a change is needed.
:::

## Health Checks<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.17+" />

The _Health Checks_ tab provides seven checks for database structure, saved data, and configuration. Click **Run Check** beside a check to start it. You can also click its heading to expand or collapse its details.

Checks run only when requested and do not change your data. Progress and totals appear while scans run; large sites may take longer to finish.

<Photo
  img={require('@site/static/img/craft/freeform/v5/cp/health-checks.jpg')}
  alt="Freeform Diagnostics Health Checks"
/>

All checks are read-only. Notification and integration checks review saved settings without rendering Twig, sending emails, or contacting external services.

- **Database Integrity** - Checks expected tables, columns, indexes, and foreign keys. See the [console checks](./console-commands.mdx#check-database-integrity) and [foreign-key repair instructions](./console-commands.mdx#repair-database-foreign-keys). Structural issues beyond foreign keys require developer review.
- **Orphaned Submissions** - Finds submissions whose Craft element or Freeform form no longer exists. Ask your developer to [preview cleanup](./console-commands.mdx#delete-orphaned-data) if orphans are found.
- **Related Data Integrity** - Finds related records with missing parents or duplicate data. The [cleanup command](./console-commands.mdx#delete-orphaned-data) handles orphans. Duplicates require separate developer review.
- **Uploaded File Integrity** - Checks upload references, including Table fields, for missing assets or files. Unreadable values and unavailable storage are reported separately.
- **Notification Readiness** - Checks enabled notifications on active forms for missing templates, recipient fields, rule references, and invalid email settings. It does not verify delivery.
- **Integration Readiness** - Checks enabled integrations and active form connections for missing settings, credential and OAuth configuration issues, and broken field references.
- **Queue Health** - Flags failed jobs, overdue jobs, and jobs waiting to run for at least an hour, including jobs from other plugins. Scheduled delays are respected. Custom queue drivers may require developer review.

### Understanding Results

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <span className="docs-diagnostic-status" data-status="idle">Not checked</span> | Not run in this page session. |
| <span className="docs-diagnostic-status" data-status="running">Running</span> | The check is in progress. |
| <span className="docs-diagnostic-status" data-status="passed">Passed</span> | No issues or unchecked items were found. |
| <span className="docs-diagnostic-status" data-status="warning">Needs attention</span> | Issues were found, or some items could not be checked. |
| <span className="docs-diagnostic-status" data-status="info">Information</span> | Informational findings, such as upload values that could not be inspected. |
| <span className="docs-diagnostic-status" data-status="error">Incomplete</span> | The check did not finish. Results may be partial. |

Findings appear under **Issues**, **Unchecked**, or **Information**. Some checks show only the first 100 findings, but totals include all results.

Use the available action links to review affected items, make corrections, and run the check again. Links depend on your permissions.

## Form Diagnostics<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.17.3+" />

For diagnostics about a specific form, open its builder and go to **Settings → Usage & Stats**. This page shows submission activity, form configuration, and troubleshooting details. Click **Copy diagnostics** to copy a support report, or share a screenshot.

See the [Usage & Stats documentation](../forms/builder/settings.mdx#usage-and-stats) for more information.

## Sharing Results with Support

Click **Copy Support Report** to copy the installation-wide Overview report. You can also expand **Support Report** to view and manually copy its contents. Run **Database Integrity** before copying to include its results.

For an issue affecting a specific form, also include its **Copy diagnostics** report from **Settings → Usage & Stats**. This reflects the current builder settings, including any unsaved changes.

Send the reports with a description of the problem, steps to reproduce it, and any relevant health-check messages or screenshots.

:::info Contact Support

- [Private support ticket](/support/#support-form) - Get help with your site and share diagnostic reports or screenshots privately.
- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/solspace/craft-freeform/issues/new/choose) - Report reproducible bugs or suggest features publicly, without sharing private site or customer details.

:::
