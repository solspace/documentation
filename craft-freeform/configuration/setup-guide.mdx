# Setup Guide<Badge type="feature" text="New in 5.18+" />

Open **Freeform → Settings → Setup Guide** to review recommended site settings. You must be an administrator, and Craft's `allowAdminChanges` must be enabled.

The guide suggests site defaults, logging, shared email notifications, and Limited Users profiles based on your current setup. Review the changes before applying them. Existing forms keep their settings.

<VerticalStepWrapper>
<StepMarkdown>

In **Your workflow**, choose who will maintain forms and how you plan to build the front end.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

In **Your needs**, select the features your forms will use.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

In **Suggested setup**, review the proposed settings. Adjust the selected changes, shared notification resources, and any Pro Limited Users profiles.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

In **Review**, check the final changes and choose **Apply setup**. Existing resources are not replaced.

</StepMarkdown>
</VerticalStepWrapper>

Defaults apply to new forms and fields. After applying the setup, open the builder to check the result.

Use [General Settings](./settings/general.mdx), [Form Builder Defaults](./settings/form-builder.mdx), [Logging](./settings/logs.mdx), and [Limited Users](./limited-users.mdx) for ongoing configuration. The refreshed **Welcome** page also links to setup resources and edition comparisons.
