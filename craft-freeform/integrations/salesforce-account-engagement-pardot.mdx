# Salesforce Account Engagement (Pardot)<Badge type="pro" text="Pro" /><Badge type="feature" text="Improved in 5.18+" />

Create prospects with standard and custom fields in **Salesforce Marketing Cloud
Account Engagement**, formerly Pardot. Freeform uses Salesforce OAuth and version
4 of the Account Engagement API.

Existing connections and mappings still work. Salesforce menus, permissions,
and API URLs may still use the Pardot name.

<span id="setup-instructions" />

## Connect your account

<VerticalStepWrapper>
<StepMarkdown>

Copy Freeform's **OAuth Callback URL**. Follow the app setup instructions below.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Enter the app's consumer key as **Client ID** and consumer secret as **Client Secret**.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Enter your **Business Unit ID**. Find it under **Business Unit Setup** in
Salesforce Setup, or ask your administrator. Use the 18-character ID starting
with `0Uv`.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Keep the default **Salesforce login options** unless your administrator
specifies another login server.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Save the integration and click **Authorize**. Sign in with a Salesforce user who
has Account Engagement access and approve the requested permissions.

</StepMarkdown>
</VerticalStepWrapper>

<details className="hidden-heading">
<summary>Set up a Salesforce app</summary>

Use an **External Client App** for new connections. See
[Salesforce's Account Engagement quick start](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/marketing/pardot/guide/getting-started.html).

<VerticalStepWrapper>
<StepMarkdown>

In Salesforce Setup, search for **External Client Apps** and open the app manager.
Create an app for Freeform and enable OAuth.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Paste Freeform's exact **OAuth Callback URL** into the callback URL field.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Add these OAuth scopes:

- `pardot_api` for Account Engagement. Salesforce may call it **Manage Pardot
  services** or **Access Pardot services**.
- `api` for Salesforce API access.
- `refresh_token` to keep the connection authorized. It may appear alongside
  `offline_access`.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Enable the authorization code / web server flow with PKCE and a client secret.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Save the app and copy its consumer key and secret from the OAuth settings
into Freeform.

</StepMarkdown>
</VerticalStepWrapper>

Your organization's policies must allow the user to access the app. New apps
can take up to 15 minutes to become available. See
[Salesforce's OAuth settings guide](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.configure_external_client_app_oauth_settings.htm&language=en_US&type=5).

</details>

<details className="hidden-heading">
<summary>Use an existing Connected App</summary>

You can keep a working **Connected App**. In Salesforce **App Manager**, check
that OAuth is enabled, the callback URL matches Freeform's, and the scopes above
are included. Use the consumer key and secret as **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.

Use an External Client App for new connections. See
[Salesforce's Connected App guidance](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.connected_app_create_api_integration.htm&language=en_US).

</details>

<details className="hidden-heading">
<summary>Choose a Salesforce login server</summary>

Under **Salesforce login options**, choose where to authorize the connection:

- **Standard login:** leave both options off to use `https://login.salesforce.com`.
- **Use Salesforce sandbox login:** use `https://test.salesforce.com`.
- **Use custom Salesforce login URL:** enter a full URL in **Salesforce login URL**,
  such as `https://mycompany.my.salesforce.com`. This overrides the sandbox option.

These options change the login server only. Account Engagement API requests
use the production endpoint at `https://pi.pardot.com/api`.

</details>

## Map your form

<VerticalStepWrapper>
<StepMarkdown>

In the form builder, open **Integrations** and choose **Salesforce Account
Engagement (Pardot)** or the name you gave the connection.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Enable the integration and **Map to Prospect**.

</StepMarkdown>
<StepMarkdown>

Map the form's email field and other prospect fields. Enable **Map to Custom**
for custom prospect fields, then save the form.

</StepMarkdown>
</VerticalStepWrapper>

<details className="hidden-heading">
<summary>Troubleshoot authorization</summary>

- Match the Salesforce callback URL exactly to Freeform's **OAuth Callback URL**.
- Use the app's consumer key and secret, not a Salesforce username and password.
- Check that the Business Unit ID belongs to the business unit you want to use.
- Give the user Account Engagement access and permission to authorize the app.
- When moving to production, update the callback URL and authorize the
  connection on the production site.

See [Salesforce's authentication guide](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/marketing/pardot/guide/authentication)
for Account Engagement requirements.

</details>
